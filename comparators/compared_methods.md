# Appendix A. Data and pretraining overlap audit

> Part of the evidence package for the manuscript *Shortcut-aware electrocardiogram screening for Chagas disease with demographic context and missing leads* (Y. Zhang, H. Zhao and J. Zhou). Appendix A is in `comparators/compared_methods.md`, Appendix B in `test_reads/test_read_list.md`, and Appendices C and D in `extended_results/extended_results.md`; "Table I" and "Fig. 3" refer to the paper. Dates and times are Beijing time (UTC+8).

This appendix distinguishes overlap of pretraining corpora from patient overlap. In Table A1, “No” means that the pretraining corpus declared in the original publication does not contain the database; it does not mean that overlap was excluded person by person. “Unknown” is used when the original publication does not list the complete pretraining corpus. Data used for downstream fine-tuning and evaluation are not counted as pretraining corpora.

ECGFounder is the backbone of CG-Net; the small model of HuBERT-ECG was used; the ResNet row describes the initialization network shared by the two ResNet variants.

The unlabeled pretraining of xECG and ST-MEM covers all and part, respectively, of our CODE-15% validation and test ECGs. This pretraining-corpus overlap favors these two methods, and xECG may also have seen ECGs of SaMi-Trop patients. The initialization network of the two ResNets was trained with supervision on 98% of the CODE study ECGs from 2010 to 2016, with labels that include right bundle branch block, which is associated with Chagas cardiomyopathy; it has therefore very likely seen most of our CODE-15% validation and test ECGs and may have seen ECGs of SaMi-Trop patients, which favors these two methods. Both are budget-limited methods and received no decision in the same-pipeline comparison. The pretraining corpus of our backbone ECGFounder contains none of these three databases. Items marked “Unknown” should not be read as having no overlap.
