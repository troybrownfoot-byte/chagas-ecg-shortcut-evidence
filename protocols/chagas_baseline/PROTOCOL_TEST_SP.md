## Basis for an inference-only intervention

CG-Net was trained on original-length inputs, not cropped inputs; its raw-record crop is inference-only. Therefore adding the fixed raw crop and longitudinal pooling to frozen comparison checkpoints, with zero new training, is the same intervention used by CG-Net. The paper will state the training/inference distinction explicitly.

The evidence for retaining inference-only cropping is limited to the tested alternatives. PROTOCOL_DSH.stageA.result.md (Stage A, two seeds, format H hold-out) reports EPCDSED minus its same-seed reference: mean V1_strat -0.0048 and V2 +0.0057; EPCDSEDX, which additionally cropped device-B inputs during training: V1_strat -0.0078 and V2 -0.0193. Both arms failed the locked and amended Stage A rules. PROTOCOL_LEN.md amendment 3 result (2026-10-23 16:15) and analysis/results/len/len_stage1.json report stage-1-only secondary H4b, training-time LEN windows C1 minus inference-only LEN windows C0: V1_strat +0.0061 (95% CI -0.0092 to +0.0227), V2 -0.0738 (-0.0964 to -0.0509), so H4b was not met. The originally planned final-model H4/H4b could not be evaluated after the stage-3 stopping rule; these are the amendment's stage-1 secondary results. LEN training was dropped. These results concern the tested training formats; LEN exact-zero windows and raw crop-before-native-preprocessing are distinct inputs.
