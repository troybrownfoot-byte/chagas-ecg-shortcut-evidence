# Appendix B. Test-set reads and hypothesis registration

> Part of the evidence package for the manuscript *Shortcut-aware electrocardiogram screening for Chagas disease with demographic context and missing leads* (Y. Zhang, H. Zhao and J. Zhou). Appendix A is in `comparators/compared_methods.md`, Appendix B in `test_reads/test_read_list.md`, and Appendices C and D in `extended_results/extended_results.md`; "Table I" and "Fig. 3" refer to the paper. Dates and times are Beijing time (UTC+8).

Read numbers follow the original numbering of the test-set usage log and are not renumbered for reads that were not executed; Table B1 lists all executed reads and Table B2 the registered reads that were not executed. S denotes the length-stratified AUROC on CODE-15%, P the crude AUROC on CODE-15%, V1 the internal CODE-15% AUROC (not stratified by length) used in the protocols of reads 2–4, V2 the AUROC of SaMi-Trop confirmed patients against CODE-15% self-reported negatives, and V3 the source AUROC. A registered hypothesis may contain several endpoints that must all be met. In the conclusion column, “supported” follows the decision of the corresponding locked protocol and log entry; it does not mean that the hypothesis passed the correction of the cumulative family or was independently confirmed.

Counted by logged batch, 24 reads were executed. The new hypotheses of module confirmation total 57, consistent with the paper; the 26 same-pipeline comparisons of read 29 form a separate family, are Bonferroni-corrected only within that family and are not counted in the 57. A read batch is not a count of file openings, and a source correction within the same authorized batch remains in the original batch. TF3 of read 10 was not tested but is conservatively counted, and the descriptive comparison T4 registered in read 4 is not removed from the family. Read 16 was called read 14 in its early result file and is numbered here according to the log. The original time of read 9 was recorded in Pacific time and is converted to Beijing time; read 8 keeps the log date instead of the anomalous machine date. The comparison with standard noisy-label methods (Appendix C.17) scored newly trained held-out-cohort models once on the wave-1 records and the wave-2 follow-up on 2026-11-13, under a protocol locked before training (SUPP_G, amendment 2); it is descriptive, adds no hypothesis and does not read the CODE-15% test set.
